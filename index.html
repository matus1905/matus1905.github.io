yt-uploader

yt-uploader is a private tool used by a single person to upload their own CS2 and GTA gameplay videos to their own YouTube channel and schedule their publication.

It is not offered to the public. It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos and set their title, description, tags, thumbnail and publishing time on the owner's channel.
